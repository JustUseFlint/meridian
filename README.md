# Meridian

Adaptive personality + political compass test. Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no build, no backend. Research notes in `research/`.

Live: https://justuseflint.github.io/meridian/

## v4 tools

- **Why am I here?** — tap a score on Compass, Traits or Morals to see the answers that moved it most, then change one and rescore instantly.
- **Friend compare** — share a compact `#compare/` code to overlay two compasses, top disagreements and shared foundations.
- **Friends rate you** — send `#rate/`; they answer 12 Big Five items about you and paste a result code back. Johari-style agreement, blind spots and façade.
- **Barnum honesty check**, **conflict mediation scripts**, and **Reframe Rally** — unlocked via Growth / Morals shards.
- **Taste & steelman shelf** — unlocked with the complete gem.
- **How you hold beliefs** — Actively Open-Minded Thinking / intellectual humility items in Curiosity & Thinking.
- Income and net-worth figures are hidden behind a “Model estimate (weak)” disclosure.
- Installable PWA (`manifest.webmanifest` + `sw.js`); everything stays in `localStorage`.
